// 04 · Process

Footage to final

STEP 1

Brief & footage

You share the brief, reference films and footage through Drive, WeTransfer or a hard drive.

STEP 2

First cut

Reels in 2–3 days. Highlights in 5–7 days. Full films in 7–10 days.

STEP 3

Revisions

Two rounds of changes, turned around in 1–2 days each.

STEP 4

Final delivery

Graded, mixed masters exported in the formats you need.

Payment: 50% when the first cut is delivered, 50% on final delivery. Turnaround times start once all footage and the brief are received. Music licensing is handled by the studio unless agreed otherwise.
